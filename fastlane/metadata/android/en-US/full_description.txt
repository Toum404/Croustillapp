Croustillapp is an open-source interface for the public CROUStillant.menu API (https://croustillant.menu/fr), giving you access to information on nearly 1,000 listed university restaurants. The app supports you every day in checking the menus of the dining halls that matter to you.

You can browse restaurants using the search bar, filters, or directly from the interactive map. The app can be used offline: data will automatically update as soon as your connection is restored.

Location access is optional and can be set manually to sort restaurants by distance from you. Distance calculations are performed locally on your device: Croustillapp does not share your location with any third parties.

All data is provided by CROUStillant.menu, and Croustillapp is not the official app of the project.
